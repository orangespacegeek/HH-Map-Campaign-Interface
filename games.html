Log Battle

Forces are the ones in the chosen system. Your side lists your own forces. The other side lists opposing players in that system.

A player can only log a battle they are in, with their own force on their side. The result is applied when the opponent confirms it. The campaign admin's save applies immediately. A loss (and both sides of a draw) drops Strength by 1. A win adds 1 to that system's loyal or traitor score. Each battle also lowers durability, then power once durability is 0.
