Campaign rules

A player can only log a battle they are in, with their own force on their side. A played result or unable to schedule is applied when the opponent confirms it. A no show waits for the campaign admin. Players cannot select No report. The campaign admin can mark an open battle No report, and a Battles deadline flags an unreported battle the same way. It stays pending until the admin confirms it. Then both forces suffer a loss, and neither side gains victory points or control. The campaign admin's save applies other results immediately. A loss (and both sides of a draw) drops Strength by 1. A win adds 1 to that system's loyal or traitor score. A played battle also lowers durability, then power once durability is 0. Unable to schedule changes nothing.
