Campaign Turn

Each player gets 2 Actions per Campaign Turn.

Turn order: Logistics → Movement → Battles

Forces

Each Force starts at Strength 3. Losing a battle reduces Strength by 1. At Strength 0 the Force is destroyed (three losses wipe a Force).

A Force regains 1 Strength if it does not fight for a full Campaign Turn, and only while it sits in an allegiance-controlled system with no opposing Forces present.

Each player starts the campaign with 2 Forces.

Battles

When opposing Forces share a system they are paired for battle (including Forces that just moved in).

If one side has more Forces, excess battles are distributed so each Force on the smaller side may fight multiple times.

  • Losing Force: Strength −1; destroyed at 0.
  • Draw: both count as losing.
  • Winning Force: +1 to their faction score (sys_loyal or sys_traitor) in that system.

System Control

Control = sys_loyal − sys_traitor. Positive = Loyalist, negative = Traitor, zero = Contested.

Each battle reduces system durability by 1. At durability 0, further battles reduce system Power Level by 1.

Victory

  • Twice the opponent’s Campaign Score after 3 turns, or
  • Control more than 70% of systems after 3 turns, or
  • All remaining enemy Forces reduced to Strength 1 (opponent wins)
