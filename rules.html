Muster

Before the first campaign turn, players join, choose Loyalist or Traitor, name their two starting forces, and pick a home system for each force. No battles are fought and no campaign actions are spent. The campaign admin begins the campaign, which locks that roster and starts Turn 1 in Logistics. A player who joins after Muster still receives two starting forces.

Discord

The campaign can post to a Discord channel when a phase starts, when the campaign admin adds a battle, and once in the 24 hours before a phase deadline. In the Battles phase those posts list the battles and can @mention a player who has saved a Discord user ID. That ID is optional. Only that player and the campaign admin can see it.

Campaign Turn

Each player gets 2 Actions per Campaign Turn.

Turn order: Logistics → Movement → Battles

Forces

Each Force starts at Strength 3. Losing a battle reduces Strength by 1. At Strength 0 the Force is destroyed (three losses wipe a Force).

A Force regains 1 Strength if it does not fight for a full Campaign Turn, and only while it sits in an allegiance-controlled system with no opposing Forces present.

Each player starts the campaign with 2 Forces.

Battles

Assignment

When opposing Forces share a system, they are paired for battle. Forces that just moved in still fight.

If one side outnumbers the other, the smaller side’s Forces fight multiple times so every opposing Force is engaged. Example: 2 Loyalist vs 5 Traitor → one Loyalist Force fights three times, the other twice.

Resolution

  • Loser: Strength −1; destroyed at 0.
  • Draw: both count as losing.
  • Winner: +1 to their faction score on that system (sys_loyal or sys_traitor).

Other outcomes

  • Unable to schedule: the players are unable to mutually find the time to play this game. Neither player is penalized or gains a boon.
  • No Show: one of the players did not respond to their opponent's attempts to schedule the game, or did not show up to, or attempt to reschedule, their scheduled game. The offending player's force loses the battle. The reporting player gains an additional logistics point next turn. This battle must be confirmed by the campaign admin.
  • No Report: no report is made for the battle. Both players' forces suffer a loss. This must be confirmed by the campaign admin.

System Control

Systems begin with admin-set sys_loyal and sys_traitor (default 0).

Control = sys_loyal − sys_traitor:

  • Positive → Loyalist control
  • Negative → Traitor control
  • Zero → Contested

Each system has Durability (default 5, max 10). Every battle lowers durability by 1. Unable to schedule and No report do not change durability. A No show lowers durability the same way as a win. After durability hits 0, further battles reduce the system’s Power Level by 1.

Scoring & Victory

At the end of each Campaign Turn, each side adds the Power Level of systems it controls to its Campaign Score.

After 3 Campaign Turns, victory if any of these hold:

  • One side has twice the Campaign Score of the other
  • One side controls more than 70% of systems
  • One side’s remaining Forces are all at Strength 1 (opponent wins)
